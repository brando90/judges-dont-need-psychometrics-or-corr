# Status 11-23-2026: correlation versus loss for judges (Exps 00–29)

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/judges-dont-need-psychometrics-or-corr/blob/main/STATUS_11-23-2026.md>

**TLDR:** Correlation alone does not identify a judge's conditional rating distribution. Through **11-23-2026**, synthetic identification (`01`, `06–08`, `11`, `17`), historical theorem-judge (`02`, `04`, `09`, `10`, `15`, `16`, `18–21`), Stanford preprint archive (`27`, `DOI: 10.25740/bh388th6008`), prompt-disjoint public benchmarks (`23–25`, `28`), frontier homework break-even (`26`), and matched four-objective `Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` LoRA controls (`29` Phase A, `921/921` GPU runs complete; `Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` `480` runs per Amendment 1 active on `mercury2`) test when and why cross-entropy (`CE`) and ranked probability score (`RPS`) beat Pearson correlation (`r`) and mean squared error (`MSE`), including mixed pre-registered outcomes (`H1` confirmed, `H2–H4` partial).

## Bottom line

1. **Primary metric & definitions:** Normalized individual-rating **ranked probability score (RPS)** (`[0, 1]`, lower is better) is primary and the shared validation-selection criterion; **cross-entropy (CE)** (`nats`, lower is better) and per-item **total variation distance (TVD)** against each empirical rating distribution (`[0, 1]`, lower is better) are secondary; Pearson `r` and Spearman `rho` stay diagnostics. Subtraction is always `comparator minus target` on the same test metric and split (`> 0` favors the `target` proper-loss judge). Uncertainty reports two-way crossed (`draw x group`) simultaneous (`max-t`) 95% intervals (`4,000` cluster-bootstrap draws) unless explicitly marked conditional (`cond_sim95`).
2. **Preprint & sister repo archival (`exp 27`, frozen):** Preprint deposited at Stanford Digital Repository (`DOI: 10.25740/bh388th6008`, `PAPER_11-23-2026.pdf` SHA-256 `6263b743...`, commit `30154e91`). Zero-exposure prospective VeriBench rating collection stays in `trustworthy-theorem-judges` experiment 09.
3. **Frontier break-even & public transforms (`exps 26 & 28`, complete):**
   - `exp 26` (`160` fresh homework answers × `5` frontier judges, `$1.34`): discrete CE-fit beats Pearson-fit on point test RPS in `35/35` judge-by-budget combinations. GPT-6 Astra (`0.0660` vs `0.0843`) and Kimi K3 (`0.0712` vs `0.0996`) pass the formal `256 -> 8` descending rule; Claude Opus 5.5 (`0.0639` vs `0.0675`), GLM 5.3 (`0.0757` vs `0.0952`), and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (`0.0827` vs `0.0867`) miss it at `256`.
   - `exp 28` (`2,350` ChaosNLI SNLI/MNLI/alphaNLI + DICES items × `5` frontier judges × `27` transforms, `~$19.69`): CE-fit beats Pearson-fit and the uniform baseline on test RPS after `4–8` ratings (`d=1`) or `10–20` ratings (`d=5`) across all `25/25` dataset-by-judge pairs; Pearson-fit leads in `19/325` combinations mainly at `N_train = 2` items, while Pearson + proper-loss calibration beats CE-fit on high-disagreement DICES from `64` ratings onward.
4. **Why CE/RPS beat Pearson & MSE (`exp 29` Phase A complete, `921/921` Qwen3-4B runs):** Top-4-block low-rank adaptation (`LoRA`, `rank = 8`, `12` validation-RPS-selected `AdamW` candidates × `10` draws) on disjoint `nvidia/HelpSteer2`, `ChaosNLI`, and `DICES-350/990` plus strictly convex 4-fold cross-validated (`CV`) post-hoc calibrators (`1D scalar_cal` vs `(K+2) logit_cal`, gradient norm `< 1e-6`). **Disclosure:** disjoint splits are new for Exp 29 (`ChaosNLI`, `alphaNLI`, `DICES`), though `265/713` (`chaos3`), `110/412` (`alpha2`), and `172/300` (`dices3`) test items had prior project exposure as frontier-scored items in Exp 28 (LoRA never trained on them); `hs2_qwen` reuses Exp 24's `ce`/`pearson` predictions while adding `161` new `rps`/`mse`/parity runs (draw-0 `B=64` CE parity RPS `0.17098` new vs `0.16886` Exp 24, `+0.00212`; max `base_probs` diff `0.2928`, `max_abs_seq_len_diff = 0`; treat HS2 cross-codebase gaps `< 0.003` cautiously).
   - **`H1` Confirmed (budget threshold nuance):** At max budget, raw CE and raw RPS beat raw Pearson across all 5 Phase A setups (`+0.01321` to `+0.07609` crossed 95% CIs `> 0`, `10/10` draws), clearing sustained crossed 95% wins from `B=16–64` ratings for CE (`64–256` for RPS; at tiny budgets `B=16`, RPS point estimates trail Pearson on `alpha2` `[-0.00204]` and `DICES` `[-0.01516, -0.00648]` before clearing at `B=256`).
   - **`H2` Partial on binary `K = 2` control (`ChaosNLI alphaNLI`, `412` test items, human leave-one-vote-out `LOVO = 0.08485`):** With `0` shape DOF (`K - 2 = 0`), raw MSE (`0.18659`) is statistically indistinguishable from raw CE (`0.18624`, `+0.00034`, crossed `[-0.00209, +0.00278]`) and `(K+2) logit_cal` adds no significant gain over `1D scalar_cal` on Pearson (`+0.00119 [-0.00114, +0.00353]`), **but pre-registered `H2` fails on calibrated equality**: `ce_scalar_cal` still beats `pearson_scalar_cal` by `+0.00429` (crossed `[+0.00058, +0.00800]`, `10/10` draws), showing Pearson's `K=2` degradation reflects level/scale drift plus residual scalar-quality loss unremoved by 1D calibration.
   - **`H3` & `H4` Partial on `K >= 3` (`ChaosNLI SNLI+MNLI` `d=1/d=5`, `DICES`, `HelpSteer2`):** Raw CE beats raw MSE on all 4 `K >= 3` panels (`+0.00305` to `+0.05508` crossed), and raw RPS clears raw MSE on 3/4 (ChaosNLI `d=5` crossed `[-0.00094, +0.01816]`, cond excludes `0`), while raw MSE vs raw Pearson clears crossed CIs on `ChaosNLI d=1/d=5` (`+0.01016`, `+0.02635`) and ties crossed on `DICES` (`[-0.00092, +0.04295]`) and `HelpSteer2` (`+0.00104 [-0.01293, +0.01501]`). `(K+2) logit_cal` significantly rescues Pearson past `1D scalar_cal` on `ChaosNLI d=1` (`+0.00127 [+0.00021, +0.00232]`), `d=5` (`+0.00368 [+0.00209, +0.00526]`), and `HelpSteer2` (`+0.00073 [+0.00016, +0.00131]`), not `DICES` (`-0.00011`). Under `1D scalar_cal`, crossed `ce` vs `pearson` CIs include `0` except at `ChaosNLI d=5` (`+0.00231 [+0.00065, +0.00398]`; `ChaosNLI d=1` `+0.00132` and `HS2` `+0.00098` clear conditionally only), and `H3` **fails on `DICES`** where `mse_scalar_cal` (`0.20761`) beats `ce_scalar_cal` (`0.20951`, `-0.00191` cond `[-0.00309, -0.00072]`).
