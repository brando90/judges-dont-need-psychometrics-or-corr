# Status 11-23-2026: correlation versus loss for judges (Exps 00–29 Complete)

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/judges-dont-need-psychometrics-or-corr/blob/main/STATUS_11-23-2026.md>

**TLDR:** Correlation alone does not identify a judge's conditional rating distribution. Through **11-23-2026**, synthetic identification (`01`, `06–08`, `11`, `17`), historical theorem-judge (`02`, `04`, `09`, `10`, `15`, `16`, `18–21`), Stanford preprint archive (`27`, `DOI: 10.25740/bh388th6008`), prompt-disjoint public benchmarks (`23–25`, `28`), frontier homework break-even (`26`), and **all `1,401/1,401` matched four-objective LoRA runs (`29`: `921` `Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` + `480` `Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` per Amendment 1)** isolate when and why cross-entropy (`CE`) and ranked probability score (`RPS`) beat Pearson correlation (`r`) and mean squared error (`MSE`), with full reporting of partial pre-registered outcomes (`H1` confirmed across all 7 panels; `H2–H4` partial).

## Bottom line

1. **Primary metric & definitions:** Normalized individual-rating **ranked probability score (RPS)** (`[0, 1]`, lower is better) is primary and the shared validation-selection criterion; **cross-entropy (CE)** (`nats`, lower is better) and per-item **total variation distance (TVD)** against each empirical rating distribution (`[0, 1]`, lower is better) are secondary; Pearson `r` and Spearman `rho` stay diagnostics. Subtraction is always `comparator minus target` on the same test metric and split (`> 0` favors the `target` proper-loss judge). Uncertainty reports two-way crossed (`draw x group`) simultaneous (`max-t`) 95% intervals (`4,000` cluster-bootstrap draws) unless explicitly marked conditional (`cond_sim95`).
2. **Preprint & sister repo archival (`exp 27`, frozen):** Preprint deposited at Stanford Digital Repository (`DOI: 10.25740/bh388th6008`, `PAPER_11-23-2026.pdf` SHA-256 `6263b743...`, commit `30154e91`). Zero-exposure prospective VeriBench rating collection stays in `trustworthy-theorem-judges` experiment 09.
3. **Frontier break-even & public transforms (`exps 26 & 28`, complete):**
   - `exp 26` (`160` fresh homework answers × `5` frontier judges, `$1.34`): discrete CE-fit beats Pearson-fit on point test RPS in `35/35` judge-by-budget combinations. GPT-6 Astra (`0.0660` vs `0.0843`) and Kimi K3 (`0.0712` vs `0.0996`) pass the formal `256 -> 8` descending rule; Claude Opus 5.5 (`0.0639` vs `0.0675`), GLM 5.3 (`0.0757` vs `0.0952`), and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (`0.0827` vs `0.0867`) miss it at `256`.
   - `exp 28` (`2,350` ChaosNLI SNLI/MNLI/alphaNLI + DICES items × `5` frontier judges × `27` transforms, `~$19.69`): CE-fit beats Pearson-fit and the uniform baseline on test RPS after `4–8` ratings (`d=1`) or `10–20` ratings (`d=5`) across all `25/25` dataset-by-judge pairs; Pearson-fit leads in `19/325` combinations mainly at `N_train = 2` items, while Pearson + proper-loss calibration beats CE-fit on high-disagreement DICES from `64` ratings onward.
4. **Why CE/RPS beat Pearson & MSE (`exp 29` COMPLETE, `1,401/1,401` GPU runs across `Qwen3-4B` & `Llama-3.1-8B`):** Top-4-block low-rank adaptation (`LoRA`, `rank = 8`, `12` validation-RPS-selected `AdamW` candidates × `10` draws) on disjoint `nvidia/HelpSteer2`, `ChaosNLI`, and `DICES-350/990` plus strictly convex 4-fold cross-validated (`CV`) post-hoc calibrators (`1D scalar_cal` vs `(K+2) logit_cal`, gradient norm `< 1e-6`). **Disclosure:** disjoint splits are new for Exp 29 (`ChaosNLI`, `alphaNLI`, `DICES`), though `265/713` (`chaos3`), `110/412` (`alpha2`), and `172/300` (`dices3`) test items had prior project exposure as frontier-scored items in Exp 28 (LoRA never trained on them); `hs2_qwen` reuses Exp 24's `ce`/`pearson` predictions while adding `161` new `rps`/`mse`/parity runs (draw-0 `B=64` CE parity RPS `0.17098` new vs `0.16886` Exp 24, `+0.00212`; max `base_probs` diff `0.2928`, `max_abs_seq_len_diff = 0`; treat HS2 cross-codebase gaps `< 0.003` cautiously).
   - **`H1` Confirmed across all `7/7` setups (`Qwen3-4B` + `Llama-3.1-8B`):** At max budget, raw CE and raw RPS beat raw Pearson across all 7 panels (`+0.01321` to `+0.07609` crossed 95% CIs `> 0`; CE `10/10` draws, RPS `>= 9/10` draws [`9/10` on `alpha2_qwen` & `chaos3_qwen d=5`, `10/10` on remaining `5`]): `chaos3_llama` `0.15546` CE / `0.15475` RPS vs `0.18982` Pearson (`+0.03435 [+0.01927, +0.04944]`), `dices3_llama` `0.20603` CE / `0.20607` RPS vs `0.25055` Pearson (`+0.04452 [+0.01905, +0.06999]`). Sustained wins span crossed CE `16–80` ratings, RPS `64–1,024` (`1,024` `alphaNLI`, `320` `ChaosNLI d=5`, `256` Qwen `DICES`, `128` `chaos3_llama`, `64` `dices3_llama`); conditional CE `16–80`, RPS `16–256`.
   - **`H2` Partial on binary `K = 2` control (`ChaosNLI alphaNLI`, `412` test items, human leave-one-vote-out `LOVO = 0.08485`):** With `0` shape DOF (`K - 2 = 0`), raw MSE (`0.18659`) is statistically indistinguishable from raw CE (`0.18624`, `+0.00034`, crossed `[-0.00209, +0.00278]`) and `(K+2) logit_cal` adds no significant gain over `1D scalar_cal` on Pearson (`+0.00119 [-0.00114, +0.00353]`), **but `H2` fails on calibrated equality**: `ce_scalar_cal` still beats `pearson_scalar_cal` (`+0.00429 [+0.00058, +0.00800]`, `10/10` draws), indicating additional error beyond scale drift unremoved by 1D calibration (`r = 0.602` vs `0.624`).
   - **`H3` & `H4` Partial across `K >= 3` (`Qwen3-4B` & `Llama-3.1-8B`):** Raw CE clears raw MSE crossed on `5/6` `K >= 3` panels (`+0.00305` to `+0.06062`, including `dices3_llama` `+0.06062 [+0.04716, +0.07408]`; `chaos3_llama` `+0.00249` cond `[+0.00089, +0.00409]`, crossed `[-0.00066, +0.00565]` while CE/TVD clear crossed) and raw RPS clears raw MSE crossed on `5/6` (`chaos3_llama` `+0.00321 [+0.00044, +0.00597]`, `dices3_llama` `+0.06057 [+0.04753, +0.07362]`). Raw MSE beats raw Pearson crossed on `ChaosNLI d=1/5` (Qwen) and `ChaosNLI` (Llama, `+0.03186 [+0.01685, +0.04687]`), ties crossed on Qwen `DICES`/`HS2`, and trails Pearson point-wise on `dices3_llama` (`-0.01610` cond). `(K+2) logit_cal` rescues Pearson past `1D scalar_cal` on Qwen `ChaosNLI d=1` (`+0.00127`), `d=5` (`+0.00368`), and `HS2` (`+0.00073`), not on DICES (`-0.00011` Qwen, `-0.00075` Llama) or `chaos3_llama` (`+0.00006` — where `ce_logit_cal` still beats `pearson_logit_cal` crossed `[+0.00024, +0.00385]`; untested hypothesis: Pearson training may retain less non-mean logit signal on Llama).
