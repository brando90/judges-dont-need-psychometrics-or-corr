\section{Planned Case Study: Judging Theorem-Proving Agents}
\label{sec:case}
We are collecting human ratings of whether theorem-proving agents produce good formal theorems relative to a reference ($y^*$). We plan to (i) fit LLM judges by Eq.~\ref{eq:kl} and by correlation maximization, (ii) compare them on held-out items using KL, normalized MSE, and correlation, and (iii) study how many human-rated pairs are needed for the KL estimate to stabilize. No results are reported here.
