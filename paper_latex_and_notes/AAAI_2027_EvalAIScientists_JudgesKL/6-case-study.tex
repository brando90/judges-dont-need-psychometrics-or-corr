\section{Human-Rating Evidence: Retrospective Theorem Judges and Public Benchmarks}
\label{sec:case}
On 75 retrospective theorem-proving artifacts (25 tasks, 19 families, 195 expert ratings~\citep{companion2026}), cached 3-call discrete histograms were inconclusive across 25 family folds (CE vs.\ Spearman $8.868$ vs.\ $8.098$, $+0.770$ [$-0.895$, $1.317$]; weighted fits~\citep{companionweighted2026} converged at $-0.010409$ [$-0.061911$, $+0.005933$]), and pooled 3-call scalar mixtures lost to constants on CE because empty bins hit $10^{-6}$ ($+0.0405$ [$+0.0237$, $+0.0575$] mixture RPS vs.\ $-0.0317$ [$-0.0638$, $-0.0047$] single-judge across 10,000 splits).

\paragraph{Native-probability open-weight and frontier theorem judges.}
Across the same 75 artifacts (60/75 reference hashes differ from annotation metadata), native six-category probabilities improve mixtures over Pearson across 10 open-weight models (200 15/4 splits: RPS $+0.0417$ [$+0.0194$, $+0.0666$], CE $1.63$ vs.\ $3.48$ (earlier text; corrected 11-26-2026: $1.63$ vs.\ $1.80$ for verbal mixtures; $3.48$ is scalar-vote CE); 59 harnesses $+0.0511$ [$+0.0222$, $+0.0833$]) and 15 frontier configurations (7 models, $5\times 5$ splits: $+0.0775$ [$+0.0437$, $+0.1135$], $0.114$ vs.\ $0.191$ RPS). Five-fold LoRA CE beats Pearson LoRA on 4B ($+0.1344$ [$+0.0495$, $+0.2220$]) and 7B ($+0.2088$ [$+0.0957$, $+0.3037$]) judges (a 12-param CE head on Pearson 4B features hits $0.141$ RPS), and 48-weight heads favor CE from 5 families ($\approx 52$ ratings; $+0.0426$ and $+0.0250$), while coarse single-prompt search stays noisy ($-0.0261$ open-weight; $-0.0060$ [$-0.0493$, $+0.0613$] joint frontier, 7/30 uniform fallbacks; obligation auditing lowers single-model RPS from $0.141$--$0.183$ to $0.114$--$0.160$).

\paragraph{Public benchmarks and open-weight LoRA scaling.}
In prompt-disjoint sets (Exp.~23), discrete CE cleared Pearson from $12$ crowd grades on KonIQ ($2{,}015$ images; $24$ crossed; $0.1068$ vs.\ $0.3072$ RPS at $6{,}144$ ratings despite $r=0.685$ vs.\ $0.645$), whereas unseen SummEval bins ($40$ articles) favored Pearson until Jeffreys pseudocounts ($\ge 0.01$) and 4-bin calibration matched CE ($0.0543$, $0.0922$ RPS). Top-4-block LoRA ($\text{rank}=8$, \texttt{AdamW}, $S_{\text{val}}$-RPS, $S_{\text{cal}}$ cal) on \texttt{HelpSteer2}~\citep{wang2024helpsteer2} ($1{,}038$ items, LOVO $0.14567$) shows raw Pearson losing on \texttt{Qwen3-4B} (Exp.~24; Exp.~25 $B\le 8{,}192$: $0.15119$ vs.\ $0.17090$, $p=0.0002$) and \texttt{Llama-3.1-8B} ($0.16031$ vs.\ $0.19409$); $S_{\text{cal}}$ keeps Qwen CE ahead at $B\ge 64$ ($0.15200$ vs.\ $0.15309$, $9/10$) until $N_{\text{cal}}\to 2{,}048$ ties RPS ($+0.00009$ [$-0.00117,+0.00136$]) and Llama ($0.16005$ vs.\ $0.15893$).
\paragraph{Frontier break-even and shape control (Exps.~26, 28, 29).}
Across five frontier judges on $160$ homework items (Exp.~26), CE-fit beats Pearson-fit in $35/35$ pairs (GPT-6 Astra, Kimi K3 pass $256\to 8$); on \texttt{ChaosNLI}~\citep{nie2020chaosnli} and \texttt{DICES-350/990}~\citep{aroyo2023dices} (Exp.~28), CE clears Pearson after $4$--$8$ ($d=1$) or $10$--$20$ ($d=5$) ratings in all $25/25$ dataset--judge pairs (earlier text: Pearson+cal wins DICES $\ge 64$; corrected 11-26-2026: the sustained mean RPS advantage at $d=1$ starts at 64 ratings for DICES-350 and 128 for DICES-990, averaging five judges). Matched \texttt{Qwen3-4B} and \texttt{Llama-3.1-8B} LoRA (Exp.~29, $1{,}401/1{,}401$ runs; $4{,}000$-draw crossed $95\%$ sim.\ bands, comparator minus CE; Exp.~28 frontier-scored $265/713$, $110/412$, $172/300$ test items, LoRA never trained on them) shows: on $K=2$ \texttt{alphaNLI} ($0$ shape DOF), MSE vs.\ CE raw RPS is indistinct ($0.18659$ vs.\ $0.18624$, [$-0.00209,+0.00278$]), whereas Pearson loses raw ($0.20820$, [$+0.01168,+0.03223$]) and under 1D cal ($+0.00429$ [$+0.00058,+0.00800$]). For $K\ge 3$, raw CE clears Pearson past $16$--$64$ ratings ($d=1$) on Qwen (\texttt{ChaosNLI} $0.14902$ vs.\ $0.16223$; \texttt{DICES} $0.20876$ vs.\ $0.28486$; \texttt{HS2} $0.15213$ vs.\ $0.18376$, CE/P reused from Exp.~24, parity $0.17098$ vs.\ $0.16886$) and Llama (\texttt{ChaosNLI} $0.15546$ vs.\ $0.18982$; \texttt{DICES} $0.20603$ vs.\ $0.25055$) and beats MSE crossed on $5/6$ $K\ge 3$ panels; logit beats 1D cal on Qwen \texttt{ChaosNLI} ($+0.00127$, $+0.00368$) and \texttt{HS2} ($+0.00073$), and \texttt{ce\_logit\_cal} beats Pearson on Qwen $d=1$ and Llama \texttt{ChaosNLI} ($+0.00293$, $+0.00205$).
