\section{Historical Theorem-Judge Evidence}
\label{sec:case}
The companion repository reports a retrospective comparison on 75 artifacts, 25 tasks, 19 families and 195 expert ratings~\citep{companion2026}.
We import only public aggregates and preserve their limitations.
% Provenance: experiment02/results/public_ttj_evidence.json; TTJ 5aef572d30048e6ce574b39d91877c14ab9b3ad7, experiment06/RESULTS.md.
Cached-histogram CE selection had held-out CE 8.868 versus 8.098 for Spearman selection.
The difference was $+0.770$ [$-0.895$, $1.317$], using a 95\% family-bootstrap interval that repeats selection.
It does not establish a CE advantage.

A separate fitted-head comparison favored CE fitting by $-0.370$ [$-0.690$, $-0.080$] in test CE, conditional on fitted heads.
However, 215/225 Pearson fits failed to converge, including 24/25 final refits.
This limits any objective-level interpretation of the comparison.
Neither the Wasserstein nor the kernel-distance follow-up resolved an advantage over Spearman selection.
All intervals are descriptive; no confirmatory hypothesis test was specified.

% Provenance: TTJ ea5d84238f6f27bf24ddc0359409921a2e55c459, training_followup_11-08-2026/RESULTS_training.md.
The separately completed weighted-probability and stable-Pearson follow-up resolved the earlier numerical comparator issue~\citep{companionweighted2026}.
All 50 primary fits passed checks and all 900 secondary fits converged.
Its weighted-loss difference was $-0.010409$ [$-0.061911$, $+0.005933$], with both mixtures refitted in 998/1,000 valid family-bootstrap draws.
Two numerical certificate failures were retained without replacement; the interval conditions on successful draws.
It still does not establish an advantage.
Our synthetic experiment neither reruns this condition nor replaces its conclusions.
Fresh human returns were not independently checked from cloud for this report; we do not infer that they are absent.
Natural-output generalization still requires matched probability forecasts, an explicit rater population, stable comparison procedures and appropriately held-out evidence.
