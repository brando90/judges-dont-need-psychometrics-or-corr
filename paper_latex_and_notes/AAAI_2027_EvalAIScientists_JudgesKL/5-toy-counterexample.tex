\section{A Synthetic Counterexample}
\label{sec:toy}
We draw $n=2000$ human scores uniformly from $\{0,\dots,5\}$. Judge A adds noise in $\{-1,0,1\}$ and clips the result. Judge B outputs $5$ if the human score is at least $3$ and $4$ otherwise, so it preserves order but squashes the scale. KL is computed between Laplace-smoothed marginal histograms (seed 0; script in the supplementary repository).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Judge & Pearson & Spearman & KL & nMSE \\
\midrule
A (noisy faithful) & 0.900 & 0.900 & 0.001 & 0.023 \\
B (squashed) & 0.877 & 0.878 & 3.420 & 0.216 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Correlation barely separates the two judges. KL and normalized MSE do.}
\end{table}
