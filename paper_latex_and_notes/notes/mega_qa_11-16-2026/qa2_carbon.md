## Metric, split and comparison contract for numbers quoted here

Following `AGENTS.md` ("Reporting results", items 1–5):

- **Synthetic learning experiments (06, 07, 08 and archived 13):**
  - **Data splits:** Training uses `n` synthetic tasks (`n` in `{4, 8, 16, 20, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048}` in 06/08/13; up to `1024` in 07) with `m = 3` independent ratings (`3n` training ratings) per task; validation ridge selection in 08 uses pooled four-fold cross-validation (CV) within those `n` training tasks (`3` ratings per task); evaluation uses `500` fresh held-out test tasks per seed scored against the exact population Beta-Binomial distribution (`100` seeds per `(scenario, n)` in 06/08/13; `30` seeds in 07). Scenarios A and B reuse the synthetic task generator of archived experiment 13, whereas test tasks never enter training or validation selection.
  - **Metrics:** Held-out test **ranked probability score (RPS) excess** is the mean over the 500 test tasks of `(1/5) sum_{j=0}^4 (Q_j(x) - P_j(x))^2`, the mean squared cumulative-probability gap between judge forecast CDF `Q` and true population CDF `P` (`[0, 1]`, lower is better, `0` is optimal / truth). Held-out test **cross-entropy (CE) excess** is the mean population Kullback–Leibler (KL) divergence `D_KL(p(.|x) || q(.|x))` in nats (`[0, inf)`, lower is better, `0` is optimal). Held-out test **Pearson correlation** correlates the judge's expected rating `sum_r r q(r|x)` with the true conditional mean `5 mu(x)` across the 500 test tasks (`[-1, 1]`, higher is better, `1` is exact linear agreement; undefined constant-forecast correlations are recorded in raw rows and replaced by `0` only where explicitly disclosed in experiment 08's correlation plot/table).
  - **Judge choice and optimization:** Each learned judge is a 78-weight linear-softmax head (`13` Fourier features `{1, cos(k pi x), sin(k pi x)}_{k=1}^6` to `6` rating logits) minimizing penalized training objective plus `(c/n) ||W||_F^2` (`c = 1` for fixed ridge; `c in {0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}` for validation-tuned fits in 08, with ties favoring larger `c` and undefined validation correlations falling back to `c = 1`), optimized by L-BFGS-B from the log training rating histogram plus `N(0, 0.01^2)` weight noise (`400` iterations / `600` evaluations per fit; `2,000` / `4,000` with `5` starts in experiment 07's long/best checks). Penalized exact-CE fitting is strictly convex; RPS and correlation objectives are non-convex and carry no global-optimum certificate.
  - **Uncertainty:** Pointwise paired `95%` percentile bootstrap intervals over the `100` (or `30`) paired seeds (`10,000` resamples); deterministic reproduction and finite-difference gradient checks are exact numerical diagnostics with no sampling interval.

- **Two diagnostic defects found and fixed (`QA2-F2`):**
  1. **Mechanism of the `1e-6` floor discrepancy:** The script's docstring and counter claimed `toy.ce_loss_grad`'s value and gradient disagree *"Whenever a rating observed in training gets probability below 1e-6"* (`(P > 0) & (Q < 1e-6)`, which occurred in only `3` evaluations across `3` rows of experiment 08). However, `toy.ce_loss_grad` computes `Q = np.clip(softmax(Z, 1), 1e-6, None); Q = Q / Q.sum(1, keepdims=True)` and `loss = -(P * np.log(Q)).sum(1).mean()`. Because `Q` is **renormalized across all 6 categories** after clipping *any* category to `1e-6`, clipping an **unobserved** category (`P_k == 0`, `Q_k < 1e-6`) inflates `Q.sum(1)` by `~1e-6` per clipped bin, shifting `log(Q_j)` on observed categories by `~ -1e-6` while the returned gradient `Q - P` ignores both clipping and renormalization. At weak ridge (`c = 0.01`, `0.1`), L-BFGS-B drives empty-bin probabilities below `1e-6` in **`285,141` of `2,692,104` evaluations across `1,907` of `4,400` rows** in experiment 08 (and `0` evaluations in experiments 07 and 13, where `c = 1` throughout). That renormalization mismatch on unobserved bins—not the `3` observed-bin hits—is why `338` `ce_valrps` rows (`337` at `c = 0.01`, `1` switching `0.01 -> 0.1`) moved by `> 1e-9` (max test RPS excess change `0.000230`). Note that `BUILDER_RECONCILIATION_qa1.md:13` quoted the `3 of 2,692,104` observed-rating count; we leave that historical reconciliation file untouched and record the corrected count in `check_ce_gradient.py`, `results/ce_gradient_check.json` and here.
  2. **Complete `make_report.py` coverage and RNG stream:** `reported()` previously checked `164` `rps_excess`/ridge quantities and reset `np.random.default_rng(0)` per pair. Extending `reported()` to replay `make_report.py`'s exact sequential `default_rng(0)` stream across **all `424` reported table and figure quantities** (`88` `rps_excess` 4-decimal cell means, `88` `ce_excess` 4-decimal cell means, `88` `pearson` 3-decimal cell means, `48` `tvd_from_constant` 3-decimal cell means, `20` RPS crossovers, `12` tuned-CE-worse lists, `4` H1 lists, `8` H2 test-Pearson lists, `44` ridge modes, `8` figure `ce_excess` crossovers, and `16` figure test-Pearson crossovers) confirms that **zero cell means, crossovers, figure annotations or hypothesis checks change**; only the `C, n = 256` tuned-CE ridge mode share moves `0.01 (69%) -> 0.01 (68%)`.
