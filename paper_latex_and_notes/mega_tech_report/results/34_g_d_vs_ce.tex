\subsection*{Bottom line}

On known synthetic ratings in $\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$, with no held-out items, a binomial-projection learner and an add-one cross-entropy learner agree when the conditional distribution is binomial. At 256 training ratings per item, expected ranked probability score (RPS) of the projection minus expected RPS of add-one cross-entropy is $-0.000187$ $[-0.000194,-0.000181]$ (400 replications, Student-$t$ 95\% interval). Both beat the pooled Laplace forecast by about $0.075$ RPS. A lenient point mass has judge-only generalizability coefficient $1$ and, at 16 ratings, expected RPS $0.049183$ $[0.047743,0.050622]$ worse than that constant forecast, with Pearson $0.960708$ $[0.959250,0.962167]$. A same-mean, same-variance shape change leaves the coefficients unchanged. At 256 ratings the projection remains $0.020508$ $[0.020476,0.020541]$ RPS, $0.689846$ $[0.689685,0.690007]$ smoothed cross-entropy nats, and $0.486076$ $[0.485578,0.486575]$ total variation distance (TVD) worse than add-one. Lower is better for RPS (0 to 1), cross-entropy (nats), and TVD (0 to 1).

At 64 ratings per rater in the six-rater mixture, add-one on all raters has expected RPS $0.139818$ $[0.139813,0.139824]$ against the true marginal $0.139585$. The lenient point mass has $0.345844$ $[0.344950,0.346739]$, and the pooled Laplace forecast has $0.191942$ $[0.191941,0.191943]$.

\subsection*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item Items are fixed. Intervals cover new ratings of those items, not a new task or a language-model judge.
\item Judge-only reliability of the point mass is 1 by construction. The empirical result is that this forecast loses to the constant forecast on RPS, smoothed cross-entropy, and TVD.
\item Absolute dependability detects a constant shift only when the human source is in the same panel. Experiment 04 remains the result on allocating raters versus occasions.
\item Smoothed cross-entropy uses the floor $10^{-6}$. Unsmoothed cross-entropy of the point mass is infinite.
\end{itemize}
