## What was run

Second list, fixed in 5364da4 (and one more item reported only):

10. **N1. The novelty question itself.** The two identification identities are classical in forecast verification, and the paper presented them without credit. Squared correlation as one minus the best-affine-refit error over the variance is the decomposition of mean-square-error skill scores in Murphy (1988, Monthly Weather Review 116(12):2417-2424) with both bias terms removed. The sampled-readout relation, that a collapsed judge's correlation is the square root of a truthful sampling judge's, is the perfect-model ensemble result of seasonal forecasting (Kumar and Hoerling 2000, Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society 81:255-264: the ensemble mean outscores a single member). Both references were checked online. The introduction, supplement 11, the note and the changelog now credit them and claim only what is ours: the statement for LLM-judge validation, in terms of label encodings, with a machine check.
11. **N2. The sampled-score statements omitted a hypothesis the Lean model builds in.** The judge's draw must be independent of the human rating given the input (a judge that copies the human rating has correlation one). Added to the introduction, supplement 12 and the note.
12. **N3. Chen et al. carried the arXiv title with the NeurIPS venue.** The bibliography now uses the proceedings title "Distributional LLM-as-a-Judge" and keeps the arXiv number in the note field.
