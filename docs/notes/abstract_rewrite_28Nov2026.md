## What the abstract does not say

- It does not say that correlation reverses a ranking on real judges. The preregistered Pearson test is null (Experiment 46).
- It does not say that selecting by a proper score beats selecting by correlation. Experiment 59 found the opposite in all 20 cells (the correlation-then-calibrate path had lower mean regret than the cross-validated proper-score path), the cross-validated path was worse than a random pick in 11 of 20 cells (post hoc, Experiment 61), and the conclusion says so.
- It does not mention cross-entropy, Lean, the 49-paper audit, the recalibration budget and transfer results (Experiments 48 and 56; cut for length, in the supplement) or the cross-entropy-versus-correlation head comparison (circular as a test of the evaluation claim; Experiments 41, 47, 53).
