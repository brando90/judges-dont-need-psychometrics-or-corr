gain = test normalized RPS of the constant forecast minus test normalized RPS of the CE-trained head (positive = CE-trained head better, lower RPS is better). Carries signal = that gain has a paired 95% item-clustered interval above 0. Items to detect the actual gain use each test item's own ratings; the 0.005 column rescales the paired standard deviation to one rating per item (approximation, see the header).
