# Experiment 42: simulated multi-modal subpopulations, proper scores versus correlation and G-theory

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/judges-dont-need-psychometrics-or-corr/blob/main/experiments/42_multimodal_simulated_population_ce_rps_vs_corr_gtheory_start_27Nov2026/results.md>

**TLDR:** This is a simulated-population testbed, not a certification on human raters. On the polarized subpopulation the exact oracle ranked probability score (RPS) is 0.221429. A Pearson binomial forecast has Pearson correlation 1 with the bin means, total variation distance (TVD) 0.614594, and RPS 0.281693. At 256 training ratings per item, binomial RPS minus persona cross-entropy RPS is 0.059517 [0.059452, 0.059583] (400 replications; positive means persona cross-entropy is better). A lenient point mass has generalizability coefficient Eρ² = 1 and is the maximum-Eρ² choice in 400/400 replications, with RPS 0.396067 [0.395810, 0.396324].
