# How to read the results in this folder | 11-26-2026

**TLDR:** Most results here are verification counts (theorems compiled, checks passed, counts of ratings), not performance numbers. Two analyses report cross-entropy on real FSC (formal specification coverage) ratings, both labelled below, and both are references or controls rather than new methods. Synthetic laws appear only as checks of a proof against a known target.

1. **Which data.**
   - Lean and the 51 seeded numeric checks in `check_plan_claims.py`: synthetic finite laws, used to check a proof against a known target distribution. They say nothing about human judges.
   - `dataset_facts.py` and `fsc_counts_11_26.py`: counts of real multi-annotator human ratings (SummEval expert records, ChaosNLI, DICES, POPQUORN, HelpSteer2's card, the anonymized FSC files). Counts, no model, no split.
   - The constant-forecast references and the item-versus-task check in `qa_of_experiments_37_38_11-26-2026/`: real FSC ratings (new set of 100 artifacts, 250 ratings, 25 tasks, from the sister repository's 11-26 freeze or the live collection folder at the commit named in each output file). The constant-forecast numbers are on each Cursor experiment's own held-out test items (24 for Experiment 37, 20 for Experiment 38). The leakage check uses only the 60 items whose tasks are sealed train or validation tasks (15 tasks); no sealed-test item is touched. The FSC ratings were seen in earlier work: see the prior-exposure lines of the inventory.
2. **Which metric.**
   - Cross-entropy (CE): the average over test items of the average over each item's ratings of the negative natural logarithm of the probability the forecast gave that rating, in nats. Range 0 to infinity; lower is better. Reference: a uniform forecast over the six grades scores log 6 = 1.7918, and the train-marginal constant forecast (the add-one smoothed share of each grade among the training ratings, the same forecast for every item) is the reference that matters on skewed data (the test share of fives is 0.694 in Experiment 37 and 0.840 in Experiment 38).
   - Pass counts: "PASS" means a stated check held; "FAIL" means it did not; the exit status is nonzero when any check fails.
   - The ledger's verdicts are my reading of a claim against the files: CORRECT, CORRECT WITH A NOTE, IMPRECISE, WRONG, NOT SUPPORTED, CONFLICTS WITH THE PROJECT.
3. **What is subtracted.** Only in the leakage check: "test CE of the trained head minus test CE of the train-marginal constant forecast", on the same held-out folds. Positive means the head is worse than the constant forecast. Nothing else in this folder subtracts two numbers.
4. **Uncertainty.** Item-bootstrap 95% percentile intervals, resampling the held-out items (1,000 draws; seed 37 for the Experiment 37 constant forecast, which is that experiment's own seed, and 20261126 for the Experiment 38 constant forecast and the leakage check). The leakage check also reports the standard deviation of the fold-averaged difference over 30 repeated cross-validations. Counts and Lean results have no uncertainty. Unpaired intervals for the head and the constant forecast are given side by side; a paired interval was not computed.
5. **How the judge was chosen.** The judges in the audited runs are Cursor's held-out softmax heads: a linear map from 8 or 24 principal components of a hashed bag of tokens to the dataset's grade categories (six for FSC), fitted by L-BFGS-B on the cross-entropy of the training items' empirical grade histograms plus an L2 penalty of 0.01, at fixed settings, with an iteration cap (200 in Experiment 37, 40 in Experiment 38). The result reflects that optimizer, start and cap; the objective is convex in the weights, but the capped runs did not all converge, which is why 28 reproduced numbers in Experiment 38 differ in the fourth decimal. My constant forecast has no fitted parameters beyond the training grade shares. Nothing was tuned on test data.

Frozen or archived result files elsewhere in the repository are not rewritten by this folder.
