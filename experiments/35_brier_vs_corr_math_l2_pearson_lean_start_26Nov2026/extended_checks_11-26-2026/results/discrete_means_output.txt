LEGEND. Data: every number below comes from synthetic ratings drawn from known true distributions (no human ratings, no held-out human data); N is the number of synthetic training ratings, and 'held-out' means a second synthetic sample of the same size used only for calibration.
LEGEND. TVD = total variation distance 0.5 * sum_k |q_k - p_k| between a learned distribution q and the true distribution p, averaged equally over contexts; range 0 to 1, lower is better; a value of 0.05 means 5 percent of the probability mass is misplaced.
LEGEND. Brier = sum_k (q_k - 1{y=k})^2; CE = cross-entropy -log q_y (lower is better, minimized by the true distribution); ranked probability score (RPS) = mean over the K-1 cumulative cuts of the squared difference between forecast and outcome cumulative probabilities; Pearson = Pearson correlation between forecast and outcome (higher is better, invariant to a + b x with b > 0).
LEGEND. 'X-trained' = the table-model logits minimized the X objective by L-BFGS from random initial logits; 'X + held-out affine calibration' = after training, each class probability is mapped by a least-squares affine map fitted on a second synthetic sample, clipped at 1e-6 and renormalized. Medians are over random initializations and two data draws; no confidence intervals are used because the truth is known and the quantity of interest is the exact distance, so the spread (min to max) is reported where it matters.
