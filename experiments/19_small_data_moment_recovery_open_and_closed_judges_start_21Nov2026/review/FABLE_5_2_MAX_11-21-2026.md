## What changed the interpretation

1. **Baseline.** The open-weight CE fit improved held-out ranked probability score relative to the Pearson fit. An unfitted, uniform distribution over ratings outperformed both Qwen fits. The summary now puts that comparison next to the headline.
2. **Uncertainty.** The original percentiles describe differences after reweighting and refitting. In the original estimate every artifact in a split receives equal weight. Its fitted result can lie far from the center of the reweighted results. In the smallest Qwen cell, its pointwise percentile interval excludes zero; a post-hoc basic (reflected) interval using the same draws includes zero. The report discloses the fraction of draws at or below zero. At three training families and one rating per artifact, 15/1,000 reweighted Qwen differences were at or below zero. For the closed pair at 15 families and one rating, CE fit minus original had a point difference of −0.00218 and a reweighted mean of +0.00838. [Draw summaries, percentile and reflected intervals](../human_judges/bootstrap_sensitivity.csv)
3. **Distributional identification and scoring.** Pearson of predicted and target means is blind to conditional shape at fixed means. A maximum-entropy shape gap is unavoidable in the particular log-affine-in-rating forecast family. The general CE decomposition contains a signed cross term. The score the human study uses is normalized individual-rating RPS.
4. **Synthetic reversal.** At six labels with low spread, the within-curve reversal in mean estimation depends on which tied curve is selected. With the two symmetric curves equally weighted, CE has mean-squared error 0.022851 versus 0.023041 for Pearson. The three-label conditional-variance example still gives an exact win for either selector depending on the true spread and the tied-shape default.
5. **Search space and smoothing.** Both fits search 27 transforms of each fixed input forecast. The CE optimum frequently lies at the lowest confidence multiplier available; model identity, input zeros, sensitivity to uniform mixing, and differences in each archive’s instructions matter when interpreting fitted scores. Across its 75 six-category forecasts, Qwen2.5-Coder-14B assigned exact zero to 345 of 450 bins. At the smallest budget, CE chose the minimum temperature exponent (0.5) in 247/250 Qwen-pair splits and 214/250 OpenAI-pair splits. The grid boundary limits interpretation of the optimum.

6. **Timing.** The protocol commit precedes the results commit. The commit history alone does not timestamp the first computation.

With six categories, the normalized excess expected RPS is one fifth of that distance. For the equal-mean pair in the proof the two values are 2/9 and 2/45.
