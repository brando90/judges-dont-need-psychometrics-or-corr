# Exploratory smoothing sweep for the released GPT-4 votes | 11-21-2026

As of 11-21-2026, this plan follows the original zero-pseudocount results and the post-hoc 0.5-pseudocount results. The other six settings have not been fitted here. This is continued exploration on the same public test articles. We will report all eight settings, with no selection of a best setting or independent confirmatory claim.

For each of the two released GPT-4 judge criteria (SummEval factual consistency and relevance), add the same pseudocount α to each of the five grade counts on every item and normalize by the number of **valid** archived votes plus 5α. The values are 0, 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2. Then apply the original uniform floor and the original fixed candidate transformations. Reuse the original 64 matched acquisition draws, one human grade per training response, all eight budgets 6–768, 60 training articles and 40 held-out articles, exhaustive grid, tie rules, histogram, and four-bin Pearson calibration. Report held-out normalized individual ranked probability score (RPS), cross-entropy (CE), total variation distance to three-vote histograms, all four original paired RPS contrasts and original simultaneous bands within each criterion and α. Compute first tested budget sustaining a positive lower RPS band against each comparator, Pearson plus uniform together, and all four together through the highest tested budget. Positive means the CE-trained judge has lower test RPS. Show failures as well as successes. The question is whether the reported conclusion is sensitive to probability assigned to previously unseen model grades. The 0 and 0.5 settings must match the previously released acquisition results.

The within-setting 97.5% simultaneous bands use 4,000 held-out-article cluster bootstrap draws across the eight budgets and four reference methods, conditional on the fixed training schedules. They do not adjust for the additional smoothing settings or criteria. No paid inference and no new annotations.
