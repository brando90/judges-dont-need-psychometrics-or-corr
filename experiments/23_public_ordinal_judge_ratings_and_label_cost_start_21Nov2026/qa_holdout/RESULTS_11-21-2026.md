As of 11-21-2026. **The original KonIQ thresholds repeated on the 1,000 reserved photographs.** Direct cross-entropy (CE) selection beat direct Pearson selection and a uniform forecast on held-out ranked probability score (RPS) from 12 ratings when conditioning on the 64 training acquisitions, and from 24 ratings when also resampling the acquisitions. Both thresholds persisted through 6,144 ratings. **Neither image set had a sustained threshold against all four comparators** when the training histogram and calibrated Pearson were included. This exploratory second-split check was registered at `233b6de` after the original 2,015-image test results and before scoring the reserved images. The 1,000 reserved images have 107,003 crowd ratings across five ordered grades of technical image quality. Image IDs are disjoint from the original 2,015 test and 7,058 training images. Each training acquisition supplies one grade on each of 6–6,144 distinct training images. We replayed all 704 combinations of training draw and budget, matching every original CE, Pearson and RPS selection and each undefined-Pearson flag. Both test evaluations therefore use the same 64 acquisitions and fitted judges. Human votes, grade totals and mean opinion scores never enter the seven technical predictors. This split provides more photographs from the original rater collection. Worker identifiers are unavailable, so worker overlap cannot be assessed. A training draw wins when its direct CE-selected judge has lower mean RPS on those held-out images than every comparator in the stated set. The threshold instead compares **mean performance across acquisitions** and requires all simultaneous lower bounds to remain positive at that and every larger tested budget. On the reserved set, the mean test RPS of uniform minus direct CE was +.00393 at 12 ratings (fixed 97.5% simultaneous interval [.00095, .00691], crossed [−.00567, .01354]) and +.00986 at 24 (fixed [.00662, .01310], crossed [.00223, .01749]). At 24 ratings, calibrated Pearson minus direct CE was −.01873 (crossed [−.02645, −.01100]). Paired intervals use 4,000 resamples, cover eleven budgets and four comparisons simultaneously, and follow the primary study’s 97.5% within-benchmark rule. The fixed interval resamples images. The crossed interval independently weights images and the 64 original acquisition draws. Neither quantifies variation across newly recruited worker populations. RPS averages squared errors of the four cumulative grade probabilities for an individual vote (0–1). CE averages the negative natural logarithm of the assigned grade probability (nats). Total variation distance (TVD) is half the absolute probability error summed over grades against an image’s empirical grade histogram (0–1). Lower is better for every metric. Average votes within image, images equally, and the 64 matched training draws equally. Both direct selectors exhaustively search the same 217 distributions using seven technical image indicators and their first two principal components, with fixed candidate order and a tie tolerance of 10⁻¹⁰. Calibrated Pearson refits grade probabilities in training-score quartiles using the same graded images and eight prior pseudo-observations per bin. The CSV gives all seven methods and eleven budgets. ## Reproduction

From this folder, use the [pinned source adapter](../koniq/prepare.py) and the commands below. The second verifier independently checks all seven methods across three metrics and both joint win counts at 12 and 24, using an ordinal energy-score calculation. The repeated-image split and thresholds describe this dataset and fitting protocol. The use of the same acquisitions across the two tests makes uncertainty about training acquisitions shared between panels.

```sh
python3 ../koniq/prepare.py /tmp/koniq-public-source /tmp/koniq-holdout.npz
python3 run.py /tmp/koniq-holdout.npz /tmp/koniq-holdout-results
python3 verify.py /tmp/koniq-holdout.npz /tmp/koniq-holdout-results
diff -r results /tmp/koniq-holdout-results
```
