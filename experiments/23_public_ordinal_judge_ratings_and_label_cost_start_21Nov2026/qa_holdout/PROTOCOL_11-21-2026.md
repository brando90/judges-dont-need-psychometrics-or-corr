As of 11-21-2026. This exploratory check follows the published primary test results and external review. The parent protocol reserved the authors’ 1,000 validation images. Their grade counts were previously verified during source preparation. The primary study never fitted, tuned, or scored judges on these images. Record this plan before inspecting forecast performance on them. This is a second image set from the same benchmark and rater collection.

- Keep the 7,058 original training images, seven technical predictors, 217 candidate distributions, tie policy, calibration map, 64 nested acquisition draws, and eleven budgets (6 to 6,144) from the parent experiment. Use exactly one observed human grade per training image. Recompute the discrete training selections and require every cross-entropy (CE), Pearson and ranked probability score (RPS) winner and undefined-Pearson indicator to equal the primary fit's published selection before scoring the second panel. Check unique image IDs and the disjointness of training, validation and original test sets. Never use second-panel outcomes to change a fit, score-bin boundary or decision rule.
- Evaluate on all 1,000 validation images with their original grade counts. Average votes within image, then images and acquisition draws equally. Report normalized individual-rating ranked probability score (RPS), cross-entropy (CE, nats), and total variation distance (TVD) against empirical image histograms for each method. Compare the direct CE-selected judge's RPS with direct Pearson, uniform, training histogram and calibrated Pearson.
- Reuse the primary 4,000-resample, 97.5% within-benchmark simultaneous maximum-statistic bands across eleven budgets and four contrasts. Report one band conditioning on the 64 acquisition draws (resample whole images) and one crossed band (resample whole images and the 64 acquisition draws independently). Reuse original seeds 20261123 and 20261124. Keep the original two gate definitions: beat direct Pearson and uniform; additionally beat the training histogram and calibrated Pearson. Report the smallest tested budget from which each gate holds at every larger tested budget, requiring strictly positive simultaneous lower bounds. At both 12 and 24 training ratings report the counts of individual acquisition draws beating both and all four comparators on mean held-out RPS. Draw counts measure repeatability on the observed panel and are descriptive.
- Show original test and second-panel thresholds side by side. The primary test already suggested particular budgets, so a second-panel result is supplementary and is conditional on this rater population, input family and training pool. We expect the uncalibrated Pearson comparison to persist, while a small margin against a training histogram or calibrated Pearson could change sign on a new image set. The same-domain second panel supplies neither new raters nor a new benchmark.
