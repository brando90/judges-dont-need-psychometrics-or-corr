# Conditional rating shape and annotation budget | Protocol | 11-21-2026

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/judges-dont-need-psychometrics-or-corr/blob/main/experiments/18_conditional_shape_and_rating_budget_start_21Nov2026/PROTOCOL.md>

Date: 11-21-2026

**TLDR:** Use a finite, exactly searchable family of rating forecasts to vary the number of artifacts, ratings per artifact, and whether an observable feature changes dispersion while preserving each artifact's expected rating. Compare cross-entropy (CE) training and Pearson training on identical data. All data are synthetic.

## What this adds

Experiments 08, 11 and 17 varied training size with three ratings per artifact. Experiment 17's scalar input and strictly monotone true mean left the mean sufficient to recover all features. Here each artifact has two independent features. The second changes shape invisibly to the first moment in one truth condition. A separate neutral-shape condition shares the same mean landscape. Exact enumeration eliminates local minima. The experiment addresses identification and data allocation in this specific family. It cannot estimate the number of real expert ratings required.

## Frozen design before measurement

- Draw x uniformly on [−π,π] and z uniformly on {−1,+1}, independently. The four possible mean signals g are sin(x), cos(x), sin(2x) and cos(2x), in that order. Draw the true mean function uniformly once per replication. Define rating probabilities on r = 0,…,5 by q(r|x,z) = 1/6 + 0.06 g(x) u[r] + 0.08 s z v[r], where u = (−1,−0.6,−0.2,0.2,0.6,1), v = (1,0,−1,−1,0,1), s in {0,+1,−1}. All entries exceed zero, sum to one, and E[R|x,z] = 2.5 + 0.42 g(x). The sign of the conditional variance shift follows s z. The true neutral shape has s=0. The varying shape uses s=+1 or −1 equiprobably once per replication. The two truth conditions reuse features and underlying uniform draws, but draw ratings from their respective distributions.
- Use 256 independent end-to-end replications. Each uses NumPy default_rng(SeedSequence([20261121, replicate_index])) with 0-based indices. Within a replication, draw disjoint training, validation and test artifacts. Across the nine nested cells use the first n ∈ {8,32,128} training artifacts and first k ∈ {1,4,16} independent ratings per artifact. Keep n/4 independent validation artifacts with k ratings each for the tie sensitivity only. Hold out 384 test artifacts, three independent ratings each, throughout the replication. No real raters, task families or prior test exposure. Test metrics are never read by a fit. There are no free-running searches, additional seeds, early stopping, or model calls.
- Both fits examine the same 12 candidates (four mean curves × three shapes). Cross-entropy training minimizes mean negative natural log probability of individual training ratings. Pearson training maximizes the sample correlation between each candidate's mean and each artifact's mean training rating. Compute the invariant candidate means analytically. Numerical tie tolerance is 1e−12 in objective units. Break any tie in mean-curve order then shape order 0,+1,−1. If the training target is constant, flag Pearson as undefined and use the full candidate set for a recorded fallback with this same tie rule. Keep these replications. No hyperparameter selection or iterative optimization.
- Report both trained judges plus (i) the known population forecast; (ii) a training-marginal constant forecast with 1/2 pseudocount per category; (iii) Pearson with the true shape as tie choice (an unavailable-to-the-learner oracle diagnostic, still estimating the mean function); and (iv) both training objectives with independent validation ranked probability score (RPS) as a tie-breaker among training-optimal candidates, using the same n/4 × k validation ratings and final lexicographic rule. This estimates the effect of supplying shape information to the selection rule; count validation labels separately. Fit outcomes and undefined metrics remain in the record.
- Evaluate individual-rating normalized RPS (mean squared cumulative probability error over five thresholds, [0,1]) as primary. Evaluate individual-rating CE in nats ([0,∞)); total variation distance (TVD) to the empirical three-rating histogram and separately to the true conditional distribution (half the sum of absolute probability differences, [0,1]); Pearson and midrank Spearman correlation of predicted means with empirical test-artifact means ([−1,1], higher is better). Include their true-mean counterparts, Kendall tau-b versus empirical means, mean absolute error of artifact means, both means and signed bias in rating points. All loss/distance metrics are lower-is-better; zero signed bias is ideal. Undefined correlations (including every constant forecast) are explicitly missing. Track both true mean-curve and true-shape selection rates.
- Primary contrast: held-out RPS of the neutral-first Pearson-trained judge minus held-out RPS of the neutral-first CE-trained judge, paired within replicate. Positive favors CE. Show every cell and 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (2,000 paired resamples of independent full replications; fixed bootstrap seed 20261122; no hypothesis tests, p-val=n/a). Intervals are pointwise, describing simulation uncertainty; use zero as the prespecified boundary for a directionally resolved contrast. Comparisons across cells reuse the same replicate. At total training budget 128 also report each method's RPS difference between 128×1 and 8×16, paired across the same replicate. Report sensitivity contrasts for the two tie rules. No universal or human sample threshold will be inferred.
- Expected controls: identical predicted mean within every shape tie; identical scalar test correlations for different shapes at a fixed mean; oracle TVD to truth zero, finite CE and irreducible positive individual-rating RPS. With the true mean fixed the expected excess RPS of a neutral shape in the varying condition is 4(0.08)^2/5 = 0.00512 (cumulative v = (1,1,0,−1,−1)). Neutral-first Pearson may benefit from its correct built-in shape in the neutral condition, especially with little data. In the varying condition sufficiently much training data should let CE exploit z. A distributionally scored validation tie-break should also increasingly recover the true shape; neither CE nor Pearson is required to win a particular sample cell. No directional prediction at fixed total budget.
- Preserve one record per fit with its selected candidate, counts, fallback status and held-out metrics. Write all per-cell/control means, counts and paired intervals; independently check metric formulas and candidate selection. Run on local CPU. All additional post-run analyses must be labeled.
