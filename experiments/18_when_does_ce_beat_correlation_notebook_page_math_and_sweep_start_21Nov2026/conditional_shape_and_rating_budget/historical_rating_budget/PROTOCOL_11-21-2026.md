## Fixed comparisons and metrics

Reproduce experiment 10's 250 split orders (NumPy default_rng seed 20261116): 3 ordinary and 1 placeholder task family held out from disjoint pools of 14 and 5; shuffle the other 15. Train on the first 3, 7 or 15 families. Test every method and budget on exactly the same 4 test families per split, with every available rating. The set of families changes across splits, the labels remain historical, and no rater is held out. For each split, once, shuffle the available ratings for each artifact using default_rng(SeedSequence([20261121, 190, split_index])) in saved forecast artifact order. The first 1 or first 2 ratings form nested annotation budgets, shared across methods and family counts. Equal artifact weights within each split; average the resulting test metrics equally across splits. Report artifact and rating counts per split.

Within the training set, examine all 15 candidates for each objective: minimize mean individual-rating CE, maximize Pearson correlation across predicted and observed artifact means, or minimize individual-rating normalized RPS. Select the first candidate in archived order within 10⁻¹² of the optimum. Exclude undefined correlations from the Pearson comparison and count them. If every candidate is undefined, use the first stored candidate and count the fallback. This is an exhaustive search with no starting point, hyperparameters or validation data. The constant baseline pools retained training ratings with 1/2 pseudocount per category. Raw probability forecasts, data masks and selection budgets match across objectives.

Test RPS is primary. For each observed rating, take the mean squared difference between the five predicted cumulative probabilities and observed cumulative indicators. RPS ranges from 0 to 1, lower is better. Individual-rating CE is minus the natural log probability of the rating, in nats from zero to infinity; lower is better. Total variation distance (TVD) is half the absolute probability difference summed against the artifact's full empirical rating histogram, ranges from 0 to 1, lower is better, and is noisy with two or three observations. Pearson and tied-rank Spearman correlations compare expected and empirical mean ratings, range −1 to 1, and higher is better for association. Record mean predicted rating, mean human rating, signed bias (predicted minus human) and mean absolute error in 0–5 rating units. Undefined correlations remain missing, with counts. The constant has undefined within-split correlations.

For each of the six budgets, the primary paired difference is test RPS of the Pearson-selected judge minus test RPS of the CE-selected judge; positive favors CE. Also report the contrast to RPS selection and the constant on identical test artifacts as descriptive controls. No deployment margin or p-value. Interval excluding zero indicates a directional difference in the specified analysis only; 6 pointwise intervals, no multiple-comparison correction.

## Uncertainty and verification

Generate 1,000 Bayesian bootstrap draws using independent exponential(1) weights on the 19 distinct families per draw (seed 20261123). Reuse each family's weight everywhere it appears across the 250 overlapping splits, both methods and all budgets. All artifacts in a family share its weight. Normalize artifact weights within each train/test set, reselect the winner under weighted objectives, recompute test RPS and average across the same splits. Use 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles of the resulting paired differences. These are conditional, empirical-support credible intervals describing task-family reweighting and reselection. They hold the observed artifacts, raters, nested annotation masks, split schedule and candidate pool fixed. They do not establish external population coverage or uncertainty over new rater panels. The 250 split averages are not independent sampling units.

Check source hashes, exact join completeness, family-level disjointness, probability normalization, retained fallback counts, finite score counts, and unweighted selector agreement with direct independent calculations. Test scoring on full empirical histograms must agree with averaging individual-rating formulas for one split. The extension completes when all six budgets report 250 paired test evaluations and 1,000 reweighted-refit contrasts each; report any missing or undefined cases. Experiment 10 varied training-family count for mixtures/feature heads and used all ratings. This experiment crosses rating replication with family count for single-candidate selection.
