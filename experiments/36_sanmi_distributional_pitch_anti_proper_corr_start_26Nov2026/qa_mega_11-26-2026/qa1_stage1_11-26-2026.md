## Major issues (all fixed)

1. **VeriBench `R_v1` features came from an unpinned, locally modified sibling checkout.** `_resolve_sibling_text` read the working tree of `../veribench`. That tree sits at `d1c47355` but has 5,398 modified files, including 45 Exp 100 reference Lean files with lines deleted. Only `items.jsonl` was hash-checked. For the gold references, the pinned git bytes match the `ref_ann_sha256` recorded in `items.jsonl` for **75/75** items, while the working tree matches only **15/75**. So the `R_v1`, `R_v1` expert3, `R_v1` binary and pooled results used non-canonical artifact texts that a fresh clone could not reproduce.
   - **Fix:** `_resolve_sibling_text` now reads `git show <rev>:<path>` from the sibling checkout. It falls back to the working-tree file and then to the pinned raw.githubusercontent URL; the cache path now includes the revision. `load_veribench_fsc_rv1` now checks every Python, gold and candidate file against the SHA-256 recorded in `items.jsonl`.
   - **Effect:** I reran `run_all.py`. `R_v1` held-out test CE is now 1.737 for the CE-trained head versus 11.011 for the Pearson-trained head (was 1.740 vs 10.623). Pooled is now 1.276 versus 6.869 (was 1.279 vs 8.007). The CE-trained head still beats the Pearson-trained head on CE, sqrt(RPS) and Brier on every VeriBench ordinal view and at every k on the crossover curves.
   - `native_main` texts were identical either way, because TTJ Exp 09 is clean at `460466dd`, and cert-judge is clean.
2. **The bottom line of `SANMI_PITCH.md` overclaimed.** It said "On ALL scored … datasets … CE training or calibration consistently beats correlation … on proper scores … at every budget." Three facts contradict this:
   - On `popquorn_politeness_binary`, the Pearson-trained head is better on point estimates (CE 0.646 vs 0.652; sqrt(RPS) and Brier also).
   - Experiment 39 CE-fit beats Pearson-fit on RPS in only 40/56 configs.
   - All 3 Experiment 67 judge families have a dRPS 95% interval (CE-fit minus Pearson-fit RPS) that includes 0.

   **Fix:** the claim is now computed from `results.json` and states the scope: 9 of 9 ordinal and 3-class views, plus every ordinal and 3-class crossover curve. The binary exceptions are listed, and the E39 RPS win count and the Exp 67 RPS-interval count are stated. Step 4 and the ready-to-send note to Sanmi are scoped the same way.
3. **Wrong hardcoded count:** "MSE is finite and competitive on 5 of 6 binary sets". There are 7 binary views, and MSE CE is finite on 6 of them. The sentence is now computed from `results.json`. The other hardcoded held-out CE numbers (SummEval, DICES-990, ChaosNLI) and the test-item counts in that paragraph are computed too.
4. **Row 36 in `experiments/README.md` did not match `results.json`.** It gave POPQUORN Politeness Pearson CE as 9.548; `results.json` has 9.547455, which is 9.547. The `R_v1` and pooled values were also updated after fix 1. A new test, `test_experiments_index_row36_matches_results_json`, now guards these numbers.
