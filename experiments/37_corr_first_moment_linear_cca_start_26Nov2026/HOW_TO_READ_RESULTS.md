# How to read these results

**Which data.** Prompt-level split, seed 37, fractions 0.60 train / 0.20 validation / 0.20 test. A prompt group never crosses a split. SummEval groups are source articles, so every system and aspect of one article stays together. ChaosNLI and DICES-990 groups are the items themselves. VeriBench FSC groups are task folders. Validation is unused for fitting and for choosing hyperparameters. Rows marked "full collection" use every item, including train, and are descriptions of the ratings rather than held-out judge scores. SummEval, ChaosNLI, and DICES-990 already appear in experiments 28, 29, and 36. The VeriBench numbers are the first scoring of the experiment 09 export in this repository. There is no `R_v1` file. The export has 262 numeric scores on 106 items, median 2 ratings.

**Which metric.** Cross-entropy is `-log q(y)` in nats, lower better, uniform reference `log(K)`. Brier is the squared Euclidean distance to the one-hot rating, lower better, range [0, 2]. Ranked probability score (RPS) sums squared errors of the cumulative probabilities on the ordered classes, lower better, range [0, K-1]. `sqrt(RPS)` is the square root of the unbiased RPS, not the RPS of a square root. Total variation distance (TVD) is the plug-in `0.5 * sum_r |q(r) - p_hat(r)|` against the item's empirical rating distribution, lower better, range [0, 1]. Pearson `r` and Spearman `rho` are higher-means-more-agreement, range [-1, 1]. Canonical correlation is the largest correlation between linear projections of two views, range [0, 1].

**What is subtracted.** "Collapsed mean minus raw" is the Pearson correlation of the within-prompt mean against the held-out ratings, minus the Pearson correlation of the raw predictor ratings against those same ratings. Positive means the collapse raised the correlation. "Point mass minus add-one" is the proper score of a floored point mass at the nearest class, minus the proper score of the add-one probability mass function of the predictor ratings, on the held-out ratings. Positive means the point mass is worse. Reversal, roll, sign flip, and orthogonal mix are compared with the original cross-entropy head on the same test items.

**Uncertainty.** Percentile 95% interval. The resample draws whole items with replacement, B = 1000, seed 37. The p-value is two-sided on those draws against the null value 0. Canonical-correlation differences under an invertible map are algebraic identities; the table reports the maximum absolute numerical difference, not a confidence interval.

**How the judge was chosen.** Search space: softmax linear head on 8 principal components of a 64-dimensional signed token hash, plus an intercept, components fit on train items only. Objective: mean cross-entropy of the train items' empirical distributions, plus L2 penalty 0.01 on the non-intercept weights. Optimizer: L-BFGS-B from the zero vector, cap 200 iterations. The logit loss is convex and every fit returned success in at most 19 iterations. No validation search. VeriBench features are the task folder and the candidate filename.
