## Question

Does fitting a frozen judge's reported grade probabilities by cross-entropy (CE) beat fitting them by Pearson correlation, and beat the uniform distribution, on held-out ranked probability score (RPS) when the judge is a frontier model? Lower RPS is better. Tested on the same ten held-out homework assignments, the same 160 prompts and the same 200 seeded training draws per assignment as experiment 21, from four training ratings up.

## Data and prompts

University of North Texas short-answer grading, ten homework assignments, 160 student answers, two graders per answer (grades 0 to 5). ## Analysis (frozen)

Everything in experiment 21's protocol applies unchanged: ten leave-one-assignment-out folds, 200 training draws per fold from `SeedSequence([20261121, held-out assignment, draw])`, the 27 calibration transforms (smoothing 0.000006, t in {0.5,1,2}, b and c in {-1,0,1}), exhaustive search, ties to the setting closest to identity, CE fitted on individual training grades, Pearson fitted on training-answer mean grades (an all-undefined fit returns the original setting), budgets 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 and 256 individual ratings, comparators uniform and the training-grade histogram (0.5 pseudocount), and metrics test RPS (primary), test CE and test total variation distance (TVD). The comparison is "test RPS of the comparator minus test RPS of the CE-fitted judge on the same answers; positive means the CE-fitted judge is better". Intervals: one-sided bounds from the fold-overlap-adjusted t interval over ten fold means (standard error = standard deviation of the ten fold differences times sqrt(1/10 + 1/9), 9 degrees of freedom).

Confirmatory rule per new judge: test descending budgets 256, 128, ..., 4; a budget passes only if both lower bounds (versus Pearson and versus uniform) exceed zero; stop formal claims at the first failure. Five new judges, so one-sided alpha 0.01 each (Bonferroni over the five). Anything after the first failure, TVD, CE, the histogram comparison and the one-grade-per-answer condition are descriptive. Every judge is reported, including each budget where the Pearson-fitted judge wins.

Any disagreement is reported with its cause, not removed.
