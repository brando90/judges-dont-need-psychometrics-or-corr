## 1. Research Questions & Pre-Registered Falsifiable Hypotheses

Across Experiments 24–25 on `nvidia/HelpSteer2`, raw Pearson LoRA lagged badly behind CE LoRA on held-out RPS, yet a 7-parameter post-hoc CE calibrator carrying full pretrained logits (`tau * ell`) nearly equalized calibrated Pearson LoRA on `Qwen3-4B` (`+0.00009` at `B_total = 8,192`) and `Llama-3.1-8B` (`-0.00112`). Experiment 28 (`ChaosNLI` + `DICES`) fitted only a frozen 27-transform head without training backbone weights. Experiment 29 resolves four mechanisms with real LoRA weight updates:

1. **H1 (Four-objective ranking on deep-disagreement $K=3$ corpora):** On `ChaosNLI SNLI+MNLI-m` (`100` votes/item) and `DICES-350/990` (`69–123` votes/item), uncalibrated **CE** and **RPS** LoRA beat uncalibrated **Pearson** LoRA across budgets (`d = 1` and equal-cost `d = 5`), while direct **MSE** LoRA removes Pearson's affine level/scale blindness yet still trails **CE** and **RPS** LoRA whenever conditional disagreement shape ($v_i = \operatorname{Var}_{h_i}(Y)$) varies across items (`high_dis` vs `low_dis` slices).
2. **H2 (Binary $K=2$ dimensionality control on `ChaosNLI alphaNLI`):** When $K = 2$ ($K - 2 = 0$ shape degrees of freedom), the conditional mean $\mu(x) = q_1(x)$ uniquely determines the full probability vector. Therefore raw **MSE**, `1D scalar_cal(Pearson)`, and `1D scalar_cal(MSE)` match **CE** and **RPS** LoRA at large budgets (`B_total = 1,024`), whereas raw **Pearson** still fails due to uncalibrated level and scale.
3. **H3 (True 1D `scalar_cal` vs pretrained-logit `logit_cal` separation):** Calibrating strictly from the trained judge's 1-dimensional expected score $\hat{\mu}(x)$ (`tau = 0`) leaves calibrated **CE** and **RPS** LoRA strictly ahead of `scalar_cal(Pearson)` and `scalar_cal(MSE)` on $K = 3$ multi-rater corpora once shape learning converges, isolating how much of Experiment 24–25's equalization came from passing frozen pretrained logits `tau * ell` vs genuine scalar quality.
4. **H4 (`HelpSteer2` missing `RPS` and `MSE` LoRA baselines):** Training `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` with direct **RPS** and **MSE** LoRA on the identical Experiment 24 `4,238`-item bundle and `10` draws tests whether training directly on the primary evaluation metric (**RPS**) matches **CE** LoRA and whether direct **MSE** improves on raw **Pearson** without needing post-hoc calibration.

## 3. Backbones, Four Objectives, and Sanity Verification

- **Backbones (local snapshots on `mercury2`):** `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` (LoRA `rank = 8`, `alpha = 16.0` on top `4` blocks `32..35` + `float32` $K$-logit head initialized from `lm_head` digit tokens `"0"..str(K-1)`) and `unsloth/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` (LoRA on top `4` blocks `28..31` + $K$-logit head, explicitly preserving trailing space in `" ... grade (0-{K-1}): "` before tokenization). `MAX_SEQ_LEN = 2048` for Suites 1–3 and `4096` for Suite 4; left-padded `CHUNK_SIZE = 8` under explicit 4D `bfloat16` causal masks.
- **Matched optimization across all four objectives (`ce`, `rps`, `mse`, `pearson`):**
  Let $q_i = \operatorname{softmax}(z_i) \in \Delta^{K-1}$, grid $g = (0, 1, \dots, K-1)^\top$, expected judge score $\mu_i = q_i^\top g$, empirical training distribution $p_i$ with empirical mean $h_i = p_i^\top g$:
  - `ce`: $\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^m (-\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} p_{ik}\log q_{ik})$.
  - `rps`: $\frac{1}{m(K-1)}\sum_{i=1}^m \sum_{j=0}^{K-2} (Q_{ij} - P_{ij})^2$ where $Q_{ij}=\sum_{k\le j} q_{ik}$, $P_{ij}=\sum_{k\le j} p_{ik}$.
  - `mse`: $\frac{1}{m}\sum_{i=1}^m (\mu_i - h_i)^2$.
  - `pearson`: $-r(\mu, h)$ across the mini-batch (`PEARSON_STD_TOL = 1e-6`, exact two-pass chain rule verified; skip optimizer step if degenerate).
  All four use identical `AdamW` (`lr in {5e-5, 2e-4, 8e-4}`, `weight_decay = 0.01`, `grad_clip = 1.0`, mini-batch $M = \min(N_{\text{train}}, 32)$, steps $T(N_{\text{train}}) = \max(16, \min(160, 6\lceil N_{\text{train}}/32\rceil))$), evaluating `4` checkpoints at `{0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00} * T` and selecting the checkpoint minimizing validation **RPS** on disjoint $S_{\text{val}}$.
- **Mandatory pre-training sanity checks (`--phase sanity`):** Finite-difference vs analytical gradient check (`< 1e-6` relative error) for all 4 objectives across $K \in \{2, 3, 5\}$, 2-step batched cached-$H^{(\text{split})}$ vs live full-model equivalence (`< 1e-5` max probability diff on both backbones), single-token digit check, and one-batch 40-step overfit diagnostic.

## 4. Calibration Regimes, Metrics, and Uncertainty

Each trained judge and unmodified base model is evaluated on sealed $S_{\text{test}}$ under three calibration regimes fitted cleanly on disjoint $S_{\text{cal}}$:
1. `raw`: uncalibrated softmax probabilities $q_i$.
2. `scalar_cal`: strictly 1D score-then-calibrate (`tau = 0`), fitting $\operatorname{softmax}(a_k + b_1 m_i u_k + b_2 m_i u_k^2)$ with $K+1$ free parameters ($K-1$ zero-sum intercepts $a_k$ anchored to empirical training/cal priors with $0.5$ pseudocount plus $b_1, b_2$; $u_k = (2k/(K-1)) - 1$, $m_i = (\mu_i - \bar{\mu})/\sigma_\mu$ from $S_{\text{cal}}$) by strictly convex L-BFGS-B CE minimization with 4-fold cross-validated L2 penalty $\lambda \in \{0.05, 0.2, 1.0, 5.0\}$ (`grad_norm < 1e-6`).
3. `logit_cal`: strictly convex $(K+2)$-free-parameter ($7$ at $K=5$, $5$ at $K=3$, $4$ at $K=2$) L-BFGS-B CE fit adding $\tau \ell_{ik}$ (centered log-probabilities clipped at `1e-5`) to isolate the contribution of retained pretrained logit shape from trained scalar predictions.

Primary metric: held-out normalized individual-rating **RPS** (`0–1`, lower is better). Secondary metrics: **CE** (`6e-6` and `1e-2` floors, nats), **TVD** ($\frac{1}{2}\|q-h\|_1$), Brier, modal agreement, Pearson `r`, Spearman `rho`, Kendall `tau-b`, Lin's concordance correlation coefficient (`CCC`), signed `mean_bias = forecast_mean - human_mean`, MAE, cumulative expected calibration error (`ECE`), and `rps_low_dis` / `rps_high_dis` polarization slices. Uncertainty: `4,000` group-clustered bootstrap draws (`seed = 20261129`) reporting conditional simultaneous max-`t` 95% bands and two-way crossed (`draw × group`) simultaneous max-`t` 95% bands, plus break-even thresholds $N^\star$. Zero paid APIs are called (`$0.00` cluster GPUs). Figures use `ax.grid(True)` on every axis.
