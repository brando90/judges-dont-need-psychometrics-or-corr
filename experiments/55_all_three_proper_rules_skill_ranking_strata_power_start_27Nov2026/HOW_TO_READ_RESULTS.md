# How to read the Experiment 55 results

1. **Which data.** No training or test split: the judges' forecasts were fixed in Experiment 28 and every item is scored once. Five public rating sets (ChaosNLI SNLI 600 items, MNLI-m 600, alphaNLI 400; DICES-350 350 items, DICES-990 400 items) and five frozen frontier judges. Held out: nothing is fitted; the base rate is cross-fitted over 5 random item folds. The ratings were scored before in Experiments 44, 46 and 50 (prior exposure, stated in `PROTOCOL.md`).
2. **Which metric.** Risk = mean over items of the mean over each item's ratings of a strictly proper score: multiclass Brier (0 to 2, lower is better), cross-entropy in nats (lower is better), normalized ranked probability score (RPS; 0 to 1, lower is better). Skill = 1 - judge risk / base-rate risk (higher is better; 0 means no better than the constant base-rate forecast; negative means worse). Reference values are the base-risk column of `key_numbers.md`.
3. **What is subtracted.** Nothing is subtracted across different metrics. Skill is a ratio of two risks under the same rule on the same items. Rule-to-rule comparisons are comparisons of orderings (Kendall tau-b), not of numbers.
4. **Uncertainty.** Item-clustered bootstrap, 4,000 draws, percentile 95% intervals and two-sided bootstrap p; Holm-Bonferroni over 75 declared cells. Strata and power tables are point estimates without intervals.
5. **How the judges were chosen.** They were not fitted or chosen here: five frozen frontier judges from Experiment 28 with verbalized forecasts at temperature 0. The matched rule per set is fixed by the encoding table in `PROTOCOL.md` before any score was computed.
